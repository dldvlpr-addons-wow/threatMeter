# ForeverMeter

Lightweight damage, healing, damage taken and threat meter for **WoW Forever** (client 1.60, 12.x engine).
Pure Lua, no libraries required.

## Features
- Up to 10 modes: damage, healing, absorbs, damage taken, enemy damage taken, avoidable damage taken, interrupts, dispels, deaths, threat.
- Up to 4 windows, each with its own mode, session, position and size.
- Per-spell breakdown, player comparison, top 5 spells tooltip, death recap.
- Your own bar always shown, with your real rank, when you rank below the visible rows.
- Window snapping, per-window size and lock, mouse wheel on the title to change mode.
- Options panel (also from the gear in the title bar), bar textures and fonts, text columns, bar height, background opacity.
- Auto show (only in combat or in a group) and auto reset (new instance or new group), both optional.
- Threat warning (visual and sound) above an adjustable threshold.
- Chat report to raid, party or say (shown only to you when solo outside an instance), copy as text.
- Key bindings, addon compartment button, test mode (`/fm test`).
- 11 languages: enUS, frFR, deDE, esES, esMX, itIT, ptBR, ruRU, koKR, zhCN, zhTW.
