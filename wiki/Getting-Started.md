## Installation
Install from CurseForge or Wago with your addon manager, or manually:

1. Download the latest release zip.
2. Extract it into `World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`.
3. Check that the folder is named `ForeverMeter` (same name as `ForeverMeter.toc`).
4. Restart the game or type `/reload`.

## First steps
- One window opens on login. Type `/fm toggle` or click the ForeverMeter button in the addon compartment (next to the minimap) to show or hide it.
- Click **Menu** (or right-click the title) to pick a mode, a session, lock the window or add a new one.
- Click **Reset** to clear the data.
- Hover a bar to see the top 5 spells of that player. Click it to open the per-spell breakdown.
- Use the mouse wheel to scroll when there are more players than rows.
